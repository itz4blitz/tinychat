Networks & terms

What tinyclient does on each network, and how each network treats third-party clients, so you can decide for yourself.

tinyclient is an independent project. It is not made or endorsed by Slack, Discord, Telegram, Microsoft, Signal or Google. Six networks are in the app. The goal is parity with each official client; the gaps below are the current ones.

  • Slack

    tinyclient signs in the way Slack’s web app does and uses that session. Slack doesn’t offer an API for full clients, so a change on Slack’s side can break this until we catch up. Work workspaces may have their own policy on clients.

    Sign-inSlack’s web sign-in page; every workspace that sign-in holds
    ReadingSections, channels, threads, DMs, avatars, files, reactions, pins
    WritingText, replies, files, reactions, edit, delete, own status, mute
    CallsHuddles open in Slack

  • Discord

    Discord’s terms disallow third-party clients on user accounts. In practice its enforcement goes after automation, self-bots and spam. tinyclient automates nothing: it only does what you do, and asks for members and presence only for what is on screen, like the official app.

    It identifies as Discord’s web client in a browser, the same one you signed in with.

    Sign-inDiscord’s web sign-in page, in its own window
    ReadingServers, channels, threads, DMs, friends with presence, avatars, images, files, link cards, reactions, replies, pins, profiles
    WritingText, replies, threads, files, reactions, edit, delete, friend requests, own status, mute, leave
    CallsVoice in DMs and voice channels, with Discord’s end-to-end encryption. Stage channels are not joined.

  • Microsoft Teams

    tinyclient signs in as Microsoft’s own Teams client. Your organization’s policy applies, and an organization can turn third-party sign-ins off. Work and school accounts.

    Sign-inMicrosoft’s sign-in page, in its own window
    ReadingTeams, channels, chats, reply chains as threads, presence
    WritingText, files (through OneDrive), reactions, edit, delete, mute
    CallsCalls and meetings open in Teams

  • Telegram

    Telegram welcomes third-party clients. Its rules mean read receipts and typing indicators are always sent.

    Telegram asks every client to register an app id, and a build carries the id of whoever built it (details).

    Sign-inA QR code scanned with Telegram on your phone, or phone number and code; then your cloud password, if set
    ReadingChats, folders, topics, photos, pins
    WritingText, files, reactions, edit, delete, mute, leave
    CallsOpen in Telegram. Secret chats are not supported.

  • Signal

    tinyclient links as one more device, like Signal Desktop, using Signal’s own libsignal through presage. It never registers a phone number. It receives what arrives after it is linked, and signing out removes the device. Read receipts and typing are sent only as your Signal settings allow. Your own conversation is labelled Note to Self, including messages sent from your phone. A cached self-conversation is relabelled when the account reconnects; its messages stay.

    Sign-inLinked as a device: Settings › Linked devices › Link new device on your phone, then scan
    ReadingDirect and group chats, replies, mentions, reactions, edits, deletes. Photos and files appear as a labelled line, not shown.
    WritingText, replies, reactions, edit and delete your own
    CallsNot built yet

  • Messages

    Android Messages, paired the way messages.google.com is. The phone stays the account. SMS and RCS go through it, so the phone has to stay online. This is not iMessage, and it is not a second phone number.

    Sign-inConfirm a Google account with the emoji on your phone, or scan the code when the phone still shows a scanner
    ReadingThe chats already on the phone. Photos are shown. Video, audio, contacts, locations and other files are named and can be opened.
    WritingText, one photo or file at a time, and reactions. A large photo may leave as a smaller copy; a high-quality RCS send is not promised.
    CallsOpen in Messages, on the phone

How tinyclient behaves on every network

  • One identity per session, the same in the sign-in window, HTTP requests and the socket.
  • Nothing automated. Friend requests, new DMs, joins, reactions and edits happen only when you make them; one click, one request.
  • No enumeration. No whole-server member lists and no history crawl; members and presence only for what is on screen.
  • No polling. Sockets push; requests trace to something you did or to the socket.
  • Respect limits. On a rate limit, it waits the full time the network asks. On a captcha, phone or password check, it lets you finish in the official app.

Not in this version

These are missing on purpose until they can pass the same memory, speed and idle budgets as the rest. Each one is a place to help.

  • Message search in the window. The Quick Switcher finds a conversation, a person or a server. tinyclient --text --search searches cached message text. Opening a hit in the window, and searching the network, are not built.
  • Notifications and a Dock or taskbar badge.
  • Video, screen share, and voice messages.
  • Signal photos, files, safety numbers and calls. A contact’s new identity key is trusted automatically until safety numbers are shown.
  • Teams personal accounts. Calls and meetings open in Teams.
  • Discord stage channels, Telegram secret chats, and Slack huddles inside tinyclient. Their buttons open the official app.
